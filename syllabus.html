SCED 470 — Digital Technologies in Mathematics Education

Fall 2026 Syllabus · Boğaziçi University

This syllabus redesigns SCED 470 around a fully gamified, effort-based Experience Point (XP) system and repositions Generative AI as an active teaching collaborator alongside GeoGebra and CODAP, for the 12-week term beginning Tuesday, September 22, 2026.

1. Course Description & Objectives

SCED 470 trains pre-service mathematics teachers to design technology-rich instruction in which Generative AI is treated as an active teaching collaborator, not an add-on — used side by side with dynamic geometry (GeoGebra) and data-exploration (CODAP) environments to plan, differentiate, and assess mathematics learning for K–12 mathematics learners — your choice of elementary, middle, or high school (this course defaults to middle/high, but elementary is fine if you prefer).

The course extends TPACK (Mishra & Koehler, 2006) and SAMR (Puentedura, 2014) into an "AI-TPACK" stance and structures its objectives around the three progression levels of UNESCO's 2024 AI Competency Framework for Teachers — Acquire, Deepen, Create — spanning that framework's five domains: human-centered mindset, AI ethics, technical knowledge, pedagogical integration, and professional development.

3. The Gamified XP Assessment Framework

Traditional weighted percentages are discarded. Every student starts at 0 XP and earns their way to a final letter grade through visible effort — attendance, participation, hands-on missions, and iterative revision. The design follows the experience-point course model pioneered by Lee Sheldon's The Multiplayer Classroom, which separates effort/behavior from output quality so the two cannot substitute for each other.

Full task-by-task instructions and grading rubrics live on the Tasks & FAQ page — this section covers only how the points add up.

If you're joining during add/drop (Week 2), see the Add/Drop Grace note in Section 5 — you won't be structurally penalized for missing Week 1.

3.2 Presence & Engagement, in Detail

  • Attendance (100 XP): 4 XP per session attended on time and in full (up to 96 XP across 24 sessions), plus a 4-XP Perfect Attendance bonus. Every student holds 2 no-questions-asked Wildcards — an absence covered by a Wildcard costs no XP.
  • Active Engagement (150 XP): each session is scored 0–7 XP by tier — Silent/Absent (0), Observer (2), Contributor (5), Catalyst (7, for contributions that move the discussion forward with evidence, a critique of an AI output, or a connection to the readings) — capped at 150 XP for the semester.

3.3 The Engagement Multiplier (how passivity is penalized)

A student's Presence & Engagement total (max 250) sets an Engagement Tier that multiplies every XP earned from Weekly Missions and Major Assignments combined (max 720 XP). A student who submits flawless work but stays passive in class cannot multiply their way into a top tier — the ceiling is structural, not a deduction.

Fully Present≥ 212 (85%+)× 1.001000 (AA is reachable)
Intermittent150–211 (60–84%)× 0.93≈ 910 (reaches BB/BA)
Passive< 150 (under 60%)× 0.87≈ 805 (reaches CC/CB)

Even a full semester in the Passive tier doesn't sink a student structurally — the floor sits in the DC range and the ceiling reaches CB/CC, so missing class costs real ground without making the course mathematically unwinnable.

3.4 The Growth Loop — Rewarding Iterative Effort

Any Weekly Mission scored below full marks may be revised once, within 7 days of receiving feedback. A resubmission recovers up to 50% of the XP lost, drawn from a shared, semester-long Growth Loop pool of 20 XP. This is the mechanism through which "continuous engagement" and revision are explicitly rewarded rather than merely tolerated.

5. Course Policies

Late Work Under the XP System

  • Each student holds 2 Grace Tokens — a 48-hour extension on any Mission or Milestone (not the Capstone), requested via a one-line Moodle form, no XP penalty.
  • Beyond Grace Tokens, late Missions/Milestones lose 10% of that item's XP per day, up to 5 days; after day 5, the item earns 0 XP unless the absence is university-excused.
  • The Growth Loop (Section 3.4) remains available on late-but-accepted work.

Joining During Add/Drop (Week 2)

If you officially add SCED 470 during the add/drop period, Week 1's Attendance and Active Engagement points are simply excluded from your Presence & Engagement total — not counted as a zero — so your Engagement Tier is calculated from the week you actually joined. You also have until the end of Week 2 to submit Mission 1, with no late penalty.

Academic Integrity & Generative AI Use

Your submissions must reflect your own understanding — AI is a collaborator you direct, not a ghostwriter.

EncouragedProhibited
Brainstorming and idea explorationSubmitting AI-generated text/code as your own
Concept clarification (AI as tutor)Having AI write entire paragraphs or drafts
Drafting outlines and structureUsing AI to answer quiz/exam/assessment questions
Grammar and style polishingParaphrasing sources via AI without reading them
Summarizing long readings (you still read and cite the original)Including unverified AI citations — AI can fabricate sources
Debugging code or GeoGebra/CODAP scripts

AI Usage Statement: every submission that used GenAI must end with a short statement naming the tool(s), how they were used, and one or two key prompts. Undocumented AI use is treated as an academic-integrity violation, carrying penalties up to a failing grade for the assignment or the course.

No Edits After the Deadline. Once a Mission's or Major's deadline has passed, the version present in your submission at that moment is the version that gets graded. Continuing to edit a Google Doc, Slide, GeoGebra file, or CODAP document linked in a past-due submission — even quietly — is treated as a late resubmission, not a grading formality.

Accessibility & Accommodations

Students with a documented disability should contact Boğaziçi University's disability support office and inform the instructor within the first two weeks so accommodations can be arranged for missions, milestones, and the capstone defense.

Wellbeing

The XP system is designed to reward sustainable effort, not burnout — use your Grace Tokens and Wildcards; they exist so one hard week does not sink your semester.
