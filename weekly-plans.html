Weekly Class-Session Plans

Each week pairs a Tuesday theory/discussion session (EF 103) with a Thursday hands-on lab (ETB 415) that scaffolds that week's Mission or Major — GeoGebra, CODAP, and AI-focused work are staggered across different weeks rather than piled into one. Readings marked optional are short async primers on Brisk Teaching — skipping them costs no XP. Full task instructions live on the Tasks & FAQ page.

Week 1 — Course Launch & Philosophy of Technology

Tuesday

  • Full syllabus walkthrough — the XP system, the 12-week arc (plus the Week 13 defense extension), course policies, and a tour of the course website and Moodle.

Thursday

  • Philosophy Essay workshop — paired discussion and guided drafting time for Mission1.

Week 2 — TPACK & SAMR → "AI-TPACK" / GeoGebra

Tuesday

  • TPACK/SAMR framework discussion. (Optional Brisk Teaching primer available beforehand.)

Before Thursday

  • Work through GeoGebra's own quick-start guide and build one basic object on your own.

Thursday

  • Guided GeoGebra build — a more substantial sketch than the take-home basics, plus a peer check. Mission2 due.

Week 3 — AI Literacy & Prompt Engineering / AI Worksheet Critique

Tuesday

  • Prompt-engineering practice for math tasks. (Optional Brisk Teaching primer available beforehand.)

Thursday

  • AI Worksheet Critique — compare a general AI tool's output with coteach.ai's on the same prompt. Mission3 due.

Week 4 — Data Moves & CODAP Pedagogy

Tuesday

  • Data-moves & statistical-reasoning discussion.

Before Thursday

  • Work through CODAP's own Educators guide and open one shared dataset on your own.

Thursday

  • Guided CODAP investigation with your own dataset, plus a peer check. Mission4 due.

Week 5 — UNESCO AI Competency Framework: Ethics

Tuesday

  • UNESCO AI-ethics framework discussion. (Optional Brisk Teaching primer available beforehand.)

Thursday

  • Major1 work session — revise and curate your strongest GeoGebra/CODAP/AI artifacts.

Week 6 — Critical Perspectives on EdTech

Tuesday

  • Critical-perspectives debate. (Optional Brisk Teaching primer available beforehand — the most skippable of the semester's optional readings.)

Thursday

  • NO CLASS (Republic Day) — optional async side quest. Major 1 due today regardless.

Week 7 — UDL + AI for Differentiation

Tuesday

  • Universal Design for Learning + AI discussion.

Thursday

  • Differentiation Mini-Task — extend one of your prior Missions. Mission5 due.

Week 8 — AI-Powered Formative Assessment

Tuesday

  • Formative-assessment & real-time-feedback discussion.

Thursday

  • AI Formative-Assessment Mini-Task. Mission6 due.

Week 9 — Computational Thinking

Tuesday

  • Computational-thinking discussion. (Optional Brisk Teaching primer available beforehand.)

Thursday

  • Major2 work session — draft your annotated lesson artifact and ethics reflection; peer-review round.

Week 10 — Backward Design for the Capstone

Tuesday

  • Backward-design framing for your Capstone.

Thursday

  • Computational Thinking Mini-Task — the last of the 7 regular Missions. Mission7 due.

Week 11 — Ethics, Equity & the Future of Math Teaching

Tuesday

  • Synthesis fishbowl discussion.

Thursday

  • Capstone Studio & "Guild Critique" — structured peer feedback on your Capstone prototype.

Week 12 — Capstone Studio II

Tuesday

  • Final revisions and defense-pitch practice.

Thursday

  • Unit plan and Philosophy Bookend reflection due; course wrap-up; XP tally preview; sign up for your Dec 17 slot.

Week 13 — Thursday, Dec 17 — Capstone Showcase (extended mini-seminar)

  • An extended mini-seminar format rather than a single class period — built-in breaks and light refreshments.
  • You'll present your Capstone (8–10 minutes + Q&A) and see your final XP tally.

Week 13 falls outside the regular 12-week course grid — it's a one-time extension for the public defense, scheduled the week after the course's last regular class.
