Weekly Class-Session Plans

Each week's Tuesday session (EF 103) builds the theory Thursday's hands-on work will use; Thursday (ETB 415) is guided, in-class practice that scaffolds that week's Mission or Major — shorter and simpler than the take-home task, never a repeat of it. Look for the Bridge to Thursday and Mission Launch rows — that's the explicit hand-off from class time to graded work. Full task instructions live on the Tasks & FAQ page.

Week 1 — Course Launch / Toolkit Sandbox (Mission 1)

Tuesday (EF 103)

TimeSegment
0:00–0:15Warm-Up — "two truths and an AI myth" icebreaker.
0:15–0:55Mini-Lecture — the big picture: AI as collaborator, the three-pillar toolkit, why XP replaces percentages.
0:55–1:35Structured Discussion — small groups map their own prior experience with GeoGebra/CODAP/GenAI; share out.
1:35–1:55Bridge to Thursday — walk through Mission 1's instructions together; answer format questions live.
1:55–2:00Exit Ticket — one sentence: excited about / nervous about.

Thursday (ETB 415)

TimeSegment
0:00–0:10Warm-Up — 60-second live GeoGebra demo.
0:10–0:30Tool Demo — create a GeoGebra account, build a point/slider; open CODAP and look around.
0:30–1:20Guided Build Time — everyone builds the same simple GeoGebra object together, then tries one AI question, instructor circulating.
1:20–1:45Peer Check — pairs compare their AI exchange and discuss.
1:45–2:00Mission Launch — confirm Mission 1 due date/submission location; Q&A.

Week 13 — Thursday, Dec 17 — Capstone Showcase & Final Defense (single session, no Tuesday pairing)

TimeSegment
0:00–0:10Welcome back; defense order and format reminder.
0:10–1:50Defenses — 8–10 minutes per student/pair (presentation + Q&A), back-to-back.
1:50–2:00Closing — XP tally reveal, thanks, and where the course materials stay available after the semester.

With ~20 students at 8–10 min each, 100 minutes covers roughly 10–12 defenses. Once the roster size is known, split across two back-to-back rooms/slots if needed — flag this to Serkan rather than assuming.
